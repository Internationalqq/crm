# PM.bi visual assets

The images in this directory were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and optimized to WebP for the CRM.

## Prompt set

### `dashboard-construction.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide CRM dashboard hero background for a premium construction management product. A sophisticated contemporary construction site at blue hour, with a modern concrete-and-glass building rising behind two distant engineers in hard hats reviewing plans, subtle crane silhouettes, warm practical work lights, and darker negative space on the left for interface copy. High-end editorial architectural photography; graphite, midnight blue, steel blue, and small amber accents. No logos, readable text, UI mockup, watermark, unsafe practices, explosions, or sci-fi elements.

`project-cover-site.webp` is a card crop derived from this generated asset.

### `autobot-construction-ai.webp`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide feature-card artwork for AutoBot inside a construction CRM dashboard. A sleek friendly AI construction assistant represented as a compact futuristic robotic core with a single luminous lens, hovering above a layered architectural blueprint and material price cards, with subtle crane and building geometry in the distance. Premium cinematic 3D product illustration in graphite, midnight navy, cobalt blue, and construction-yellow accents; visual interest on the right and negative space on the left. No humans, logos, readable text, UI mockup, watermark, copyrighted robot character, or busy cyberpunk clutter.

### `project-cover-interior.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: construction project cover photo. An active modern commercial interior fit-out: unfinished lobby, exposed concrete ceiling, glass partitions being installed, neatly staged materials, and two distant workers in proper safety gear. Premium editorial architectural photography with bright diffused daylight, warm concrete grey, pale oak, muted steel blue, and small safety-yellow accents. No logos, readable text, watermark, unsafe practices, fisheye distortion, clutter, or stock-photo handshakes.

### `project-cover-exterior.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: construction project cover photo. A large civic building renovation and exterior works in progress: geometric concrete facade, scaffolding on one wing, newly poured walkways, landscaped forecourt, a small excavator, and distant workers in proper safety gear. Premium documentary architectural photography in crisp early-morning sunlight after rain. No logos, readable text, watermark, unsafe practices, generic suburban house, excessive machinery, or clutter.
